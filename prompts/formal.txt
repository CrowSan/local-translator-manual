━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This is the most important creative layer.

After analyzing the reference, identify its strongest visual motifs.

For example:

• oversized letters
• elegant thin strokes
• dramatic contrast
• long horizontal strokes
• curved terminals
• overlapping words
• layered typography
• stars
• sparkles
• vines
• ornamental lines
• frames
• shadows
• glow
• color transitions
• handwritten rhythm
• irregular alignment
• large initials
• small caps
• underlines
• visual separators
• surrounding graphic marks

Do NOT merely copy these elements.

Explore how the SAME VISUAL IDEA can be translated into stronger
Persian typography.

The Persian version may:

• amplify an existing flourish
• create a more elegant word silhouette
• use a Persian letter as a visual anchor
• extend an appropriate letterform
• create a more dynamic overlap
• introduce controlled asymmetry
• make the title interact more strongly with the artwork
• create a more expressive negative-space shape
• redistribute visual weight between Persian words
• use a dominant glyph as a compositional element
• connect decorative strokes to existing Persian letterforms
• use the Persian baseline as part of the composition
• use subtle internal layering
• create stronger visual rhythm
• create more depth through existing reference effects

BUT:

Every creative decision must feel as though it naturally evolved from
the reference.

Do not add unrelated stylistic ideas.

CREATIVE EXPANSION TEST
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Before finalizing, mentally explore several possible Persian
adaptations of the same reference.

Consider variations in:

• line arrangement
• word hierarchy
• dominant glyph
• spacing
• overlap
• scale contrast
• negative space
• decorative integration
• visual rhythm
• title silhouette

Then select the strongest solution.

The selected solution should be:

MORE EXPRESSIVE THAN A LITERAL REPRODUCTION

while remaining unmistakably related to the reference.

Do not choose the most conservative option automatically.

Do not choose the most decorative option automatically.

Choose the option with the strongest balance of:

REFERENCE FIDELITY + PERSIAN TYPOGRAPHY + VISUAL IMPACT.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
NO GENERIC CREATIVE DECORATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Do NOT add decorative elements merely because they make the title
"look beautiful."

Do NOT automatically add:

• flowers
• stars
• sparkles
• arabesques
• frames
• ribbons
• ornaments
• magical symbols
• calligraphic swashes
• glowing particles
• excessive shadows
• excessive gradients

unless:

1. they already exist in the reference, OR
2. they are a natural extension of an existing reference element.

Every added element must have a DESIGN REASON.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PERSIAN TYPOGRAPHIC INVENTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Use the unique visual possibilities of Persian script creatively.

Persian letters may become part of the graphic composition.

When appropriate, explore:

• enlarged initial letters
• enlarged final letters
• controlled کشیده
• extended horizontal strokes
• expressive letter silhouettes
• carefully controlled ligature-like relationships
• overlapping glyph structures
• asymmetric word shapes
• baseline interaction
• vertical stacking
• controlled negative space
• subtle letter deformation
• decorative integration into existing ornaments

These techniques must remain:

• readable
• intentional
• elegant
• typographically plausible

Do not distort letters until they become ambiguous.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
VISUAL ENERGY ENGINE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Persian result should not feel weaker or flatter than the reference.

Preserve or improve:

• visual energy
• contrast
• rhythm
• movement
• scale
• depth
• hierarchy
• silhouette
• emotional impact

If the reference is energetic,
make the Persian adaptation equally energetic or stronger.

If the reference is delicate,
increase sophistication rather than visual noise.

If the reference is minimalist,
increase precision rather than decoration.

If the reference is dramatic,
increase typographic impact rather than adding random effects.

Creative expansion must follow the emotional energy of the reference.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
PERSIAN TITLE ADAPTATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Use the provided Persian title as the semantic source.

Preserve its meaning.

Do NOT:

• add information
• remove information
• invent subtitles
• resolve ambiguity
• alter the intended meaning
• force unnatural grammar

Natural Persian word order takes priority over English word order.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
AUTOMATIC SEMANTIC HIERARCHY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Determine which Persian words correspond to the most important visual
elements of the original title.

Automatically decide:

• dominant word
• secondary word
• supporting word
• connector word
• subtitle
• author text
• volume information

Transfer the reference's hierarchy rather than treating every word
equally.

Short grammatical words may be visually reduced when appropriate.

Important semantic words may become visually dominant.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
AUTOMATIC LINE-BREAKING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Do NOT mechanically preserve English line breaks.

Determine the best Persian composition based on:

• word length
• semantic grouping
• reference structure
• available space
• visual balance
• title footprint
• Persian readability

Possible structures include:

• single line
• two lines
• three lines
• stacked composition
• staggered composition
• asymmetric composition
• nested composition

The chosen structure must remain visually related to the reference.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
LENGTH COMPENSATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

If the Persian title is LONGER:

Do NOT simply shrink everything.

Use intelligent compensation through:

• line redistribution
• hierarchy
• scale differences
• controlled spacing
• compact secondary text
• vertical expansion
• controlled overlap
• negative-space management

If the Persian title is SHORTER:

Do NOT automatically add decoration.

Instead use:

• larger dominant typography
• stronger scale contrast
• controlled spacing
• larger visual footprint
• existing reference effects
• expressive composition

Preserve the approximate VISUAL MASS of the original title.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TYPOGRAPHIC GEOMETRY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Preserve the reference's geometry.

If the reference uses:

• horizontal → preserve horizontal dominance
• diagonal → preserve diagonal movement
• stacked → preserve stacking
• curved → preserve curvature
• asymmetrical → preserve asymmetry
• centered → preserve balance
• corner placement → preserve spatial relationship
• overlap → preserve overlap
• framing → preserve framing

Creative expansion may intensify these characteristics,
but must not replace them.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
COLOR AND EFFECT TRANSFER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Preserve the reference's:

• colors
• contrast
• highlights
• shadows
• glow
• outline
• texture
• transparency
• dimensionality

Creative expansion may refine or intensify an existing effect.

It must NOT introduce an unrelated visual effect.

For example:

existing subtle glow
→ slightly more sophisticated glow is acceptable

no glow
→ dramatic magical glow is NOT acceptable

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
DECORATIVE ELEMENT ADAPTATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Treat reference decorations as a VISUAL VOCABULARY.

Reuse, reinterpret, combine, and intelligently extend that vocabulary.

A decorative element may:

• connect to a Persian letter
• become an underline
• become a frame
• cross between words
• create visual continuity
• emphasize a dominant word
• fill negative space
• guide the eye
• integrate typography with the illustration

But it must remain visually native to the reference.

ARTWORK INTERACTION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Typography is part of the artwork, not a separate text layer.

Analyze:

• characters
• faces
• objects
• architecture
• negative space
• lighting
• foreground
• background
• focal points
• existing visual motion

Allow the Persian title to interact dynamically with the artwork when
the reference does so.

Possible interactions include:

• passing behind an object
• overlapping a character
• following an architectural curve
• occupying negative space
• visually framing a character
• aligning with environmental geometry
• connecting with existing decorative elements

Do not obscure important artwork unnecessarily.

DESIGNER SIMULATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Imagine that the original designer created this artwork with Persian
as the ORIGINAL publication language.

Then imagine that the designer wanted the Persian version to be the
most polished and visually expressive version possible.

The designer would:

• preserve their original aesthetic
• preserve their typography category
• preserve their hierarchy
• preserve their composition
• preserve their visual restraint
• exploit the unique geometry of Persian script
• improve visual rhythm where Persian requires it
• create elegant solutions to differences in word length
• use Persian glyphs as graphic forms
• add only reference-compatible enhancements

Reproduce that thought process.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
REFERENCE FIDELITY VS. CREATIVE FREEDOM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Use this hierarchy:

REFERENCE DNA
      ↓
PERSIAN ADAPTATION
      ↓
REFERENCE-DERIVED ENHANCEMENT
      ↓
FINAL POLISH

Never reverse this order.

If an idea looks impressive but makes the title visually unrelated to
the reference, reject it.

If an idea is creative but preserves the reference's visual DNA,
consider it.

If an idea is both faithful and creatively stronger, prefer it.

FINAL QUALITY CONTROL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Verify:

✓ Same visual family as the reference
✓ Same typography category
✓ Same emotional personality
✓ Comparable title footprint
✓ Comparable visual hierarchy
✓ Comparable scale relationships
✓ Comparable composition
✓ Comparable color language
✓ Comparable effects
✓ Comparable visual rhythm
✓ Persian typography is natural
✓ RTL direction is correct
✓ Persian glyphs are correctly formed
✓ Meaning is preserved
✓ Line breaks are intentional
✓ Word hierarchy is intentional
✓ The title feels integrated with the artwork
✓ Creative additions are reference-derived
✓ No generic Persian calligraphy was introduced
✓ No unrelated decorations were introduced
✓ No unnecessary visual noise was added
✓ The Persian version has equal or greater visual impact
✓ The result feels professionally art-directed

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
FINAL CREATIVE CHECK
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Ask:

"Is this merely a faithful Persian copy?"

If YES:
increase refinement, typographic expression, Persian-specific
composition, and reference-derived creativity.

Then ask:

"Has the creativity changed the identity of the reference?"

If YES:
remove the unrelated creative elements.

The final result must occupy the optimal point between:

        REFERENCE FIDELITY
              +
        PERSIAN TYPOGRAPHY
              +
        CREATIVE EXPRESSION
              +
        VISUAL ENERGY

ABSOLUTE FINAL RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

DO NOT MAKE A NEW DESIGN.

DO NOT MAKE A MECHANICAL COPY.

CREATE A MORE EXPRESSIVE PERSIAN VERSION OF THE SAME DESIGN.

Preserve the reference's DNA,
exploit the unique visual possibilities of Persian,
and intelligently expand the reference's existing ideas.

The final result must communicate:

"THE ORIGINAL DESIGN — REIMAGINED AT ITS FULL TYPOGRAPHIC POTENTIAL
FOR PERSIAN"

NOT:

"THE ORIGINAL DESIGN WITH PERSIAN TEXT"

and NOT:

"A COMPLETELY NEW PERSIAN DESIGN INSPIRED BY THE ORIGINAL."
